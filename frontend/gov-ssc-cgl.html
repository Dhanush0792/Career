SSC CGL Form Guidelines

How to fill the SSC CGL Application Form successfully?
To fill the SSC CGL application form correctly, complete the OTR (One-Time Registration) on the official SSC portal. Next, enter your educational details, upload a passport-sized photo (20-50KB, 3.5x4.5cm) without spectacles/cap, upload your signature (10-20KB), and pay the fee to prevent form rejection.

SSC CGL (Staff Selection Commission Combined Graduate Level) recruitment is highly competitive. A significant percentage of forms are rejected every year due to incorrect document formats or sizes.

Document Size Specifications
1. Passport Size Photo:
  • Size: 20 KB to 50 KB
  • Format: JPG / JPEG
  • Dimensions: 3.5 cm x 4.5 cm
2. Scanned Signature:
  • Size: 10 KB to 20 KB
  • Format: JPG / JPEG
  • Dimensions: 4.0 cm x 2.0 cm
