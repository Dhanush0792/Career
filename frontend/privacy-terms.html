Privacy & Terms

Last updated: November 2026  -  Version 2.0
TL;DR - Privacy-First Architecture
JobXApply encrypts your master career profile on your device using Web Crypto AES-GCM 256-bit encryption before syncing. We never sell your personal data, never inject ads, and never track which external job sites you browse.

01 // Information We Collect

Account & Authentication Information

When you create a JobXApply account via email/password or Google OAuth, we store your account email address, authentication identifier, and subscription tier status so you can sign in securely across devices.

Career Profile Data (Client-Side Encrypted)

When you complete your JobXApply master profile, your details are stored locally in your browser and encrypted on your device before syncing to our cloud database:

  • Full name, contact email, phone number, and city/address details
  • Education history, work experience, projects, and skills
  • LinkedIn, GitHub, and portfolio links
  • Resume content, ATS scan inputs, and cover letter drafts
  • Target roles and application pipeline entries in the Job Tracker

Payment & Billing Metadata

If you purchase the optional Pro Pass (₹199 one-time pass for 30 days), payment is processed directly through Razorpay. JobXApply stores only your Razorpay order ID, payment ID, and Pro Pass expiry timestamp. We never collect or store credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, UPI PINs, or bank credentials.

Privacy-Preserving Usage Metrics

We record non-identifying aggregate counters (such as total ATS checks or resume exports) to monitor platform health and enforce daily free-tier limits. We do not use advertising trackers or third-party behavioral analytics.

02 // How We Use Your Data

Your information is used strictly to deliver JobXApply features:

  • Autofilling job application forms when you trigger the JobXApply browser extension
  • Generating ATS-friendly resumes, cover letters, and government exam resized photos
  • Scoring your resume against job descriptions you provide
  • Syncing your encrypted profile and job tracker across your signed-in sessions
  • Verifying Pro Pass payments and responding to support requests

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to recruiters, advertisers, or data brokers.

03 // Storage, Sync & Encryption

Encryption Model: Profile sync payloads can be encrypted client-side using the Web Crypto API (AES-GCM 256-bit) with keys derived from your sync passcode via PBKDF2 (100,000 iterations, SHA-256). All network communication runs over HTTPS/TLS.

Browser & Extension Storage

Your browser stores session tokens and cached profile fields in localStorage, and the Chrome/Edge extension uses chrome.storage.local so autofill works instantly on job portals without waiting for network calls.

Cloud Database

Authentication and encrypted cloud persistence are powered by Supabase PostgreSQL so your account, application tracker, and profile remain accessible across sessions and devices.

01 // Browser Storage (jxa_* Keys)

Unlike traditional tracking cookies, localStorage data stays strictly on your device and is never automatically attached to third-party web requests. JobXApply uses prefixed keys:

  • jxa_token — Session authentication token (cleared on sign-out)
  • jxa_passcode — Local sync encryption passcode
  • jxa_local_profile — Cached profile object for fast offline access
  • jxa_user_email & jxa_role — Signed-in account email and role
  • jxa_tier & jxa_subscription — Active Free or Pro Pass status
  • jxa_applications — Local cache of your application tracker entries
  • jxa_extension_coupled — Extension pairing state
