Privacy & Terms

Last updated: August 2026  ·  Version 1.2
TL;DR — Privacy First Architecture
JobXApply is built on a local-first, zero-knowledge architecture. Your profile data is encrypted on your device using AES-GCM 256-bit encryption before being stored or synced. We do not read your profile data. The sync server only receives an encrypted blob it cannot decrypt.

01 // Information We Collect

Profile Data (Encrypted)

When you build your JobXApply master profile, the following data is stored locally on your device in your browser's localStorage, encrypted with AES-GCM using a key derived from your personal passcode:

  • Full name, date of birth, family names
  • Email address, phone number, physical address
  • Education history, work experience, skills
  • LinkedIn, GitHub, and portfolio URLs
  • Resume draft text and cover letter preferences
  • Target role and career headline

Technical Data

When you use our sync server, our backend receives and stores:

  • Your encrypted profile blob (ciphertext only — unreadable without your passcode)
  • Profile version number and last-update timestamp
  • A SHA-256 hash of your sync passcode (for authentication only — not the passcode itself)

What We Do Not Collect

JobXApply does not collect analytics, tracking identifiers, cookies, IP addresses for surveillance, or any data about which job portals you visit or apply to.

02 // How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate JobXApply's core features:

  • Autofilling job application forms on portals you navigate to
  • Generating resume documents and cover letters from your profile
  • Scoring your resume against job descriptions
  • Tracking your job applications in your local pipeline
  • Syncing your profile across your devices via the local sync server

We do not sell your data, share it with third parties, or use it for advertising purposes.

03 // Data Storage & Encryption

Encryption Model: Your profile is encrypted client-side using the Web Crypto API with AES-GCM 256-bit encryption. The encryption key is derived from your sync passcode using PBKDF2 with 100,000 iterations and SHA-256. Your passcode never leaves your device in plaintext.

Local Storage

Your profile and application data is stored in your browser's localStorage. This data remains on your device unless you explicitly use the sync server feature.

Sync Server (Optional)

If you configure a sync server (default: localhost:8787), only your encrypted profile blob is transmitted. The server cannot read your profile without your passcode, which never leaves your browser.

Extension Storage

The browser extension uses chrome.storage.local to cache your profile for fast autofill. This data stays on your device and is cleared when you disconnect the extension.

01 // What We Use Instead of Cookies

JobXApply uses your browser's localStorage API to store session and profile data. This is fundamentally different from cookies:

  • localStorage data is never sent to servers automatically (unlike cookies)
  • It cannot be accessed by third-party websites
  • It is scoped strictly to the JobXApply origin

Keys We Store in localStorage

All keys are prefixed with ch_ to prevent conflicts:

  • ch_token — session authentication token (cleared on logout)
  • ch_passcode — your sync vault passcode (local device only)
  • ch_local_profile — plaintext profile cache (local device only)
  • ch_user_email — your account email
  • ch_role — your account role (user/admin)
  • ch_applications — your application pipeline data
  • ch_extension_coupled — whether extension is paired

03 // Clearing Your Data

To remove all JobXApply data from your browser:

  • Go to Settings → Danger Zone → Clear All Local Data
  • Or: Open DevTools → Application → Local Storage → Delete all ch_* entries
  • Or: Open DevTools → Console → run Object.keys(localStorage).filter(k => k.startsWith('jxa_')).forEach(k => localStorage.removeItem(k))
