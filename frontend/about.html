ABOUT JOBXAPPLY

Built for Job Seekers,
By Job Seekers

JobXApply helps students and professionals eliminate repetitive form-filling, optimize resumes for Applicant Tracking Systems, and manage every application from one secure workspace.

Applying for jobs should not mean re-typing the same education history, work experience, and contact details across dozens of different portals. Modern hiring platforms are fragmented and repetitive, costing candidates hours every week.

We built JobXApply to give candidates their time back.

With one centralized master profile, a Manifest V3 browser extension, real-time ATS keyword scoring, and LaTeX-backed resume and cover letter generators, JobXApply turns a 20-minute application workflow into a fast, accurate, single-click review.

What JobXApply Offers

A unified career toolkit engineered for speed, ATS accuracy, and local-first privacy.
