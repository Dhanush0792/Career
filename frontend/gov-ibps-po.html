IBPS PO Document Rules

How to meet IBPS PO document size specifications?
To upload documents for IBPS PO correctly, size your photo to 20-50KB (4.5x3.5cm) and signature to 10-20KB on white paper with black ink. Additionally, prepare a left thumb impression (20-50KB) and hand-written declaration (50-100KB) in JPG format to ensure successful application submission.

IBPS PO (Institute of Banking Personnel Selection Probationary Officer) recruitment enforces strict formatting guidelines. Failing to upload files within the correct range will block the final submit step.

IBPS PO File Checklist
1. Signature Guidelines:

Must be signed on white paper with black ink. Do not write your signature in capital letters as it will lead to application rejection.

2. Hand-Written Declaration:

Must be written in English on a white paper with black ink. File size must be between 50 KB and 100 KB.
