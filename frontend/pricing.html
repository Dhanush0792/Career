Billing Operations FAQ

Can I upgrade or downgrade at any time?

Yes. Operations are flexible. You can scale your tier up or down from your dashboard at any time. Prorated credits apply automatically.

What happens if I hit my 10 autofills on the Recruit plan?

Your autofill engine will lock until the start of your next 30-day cycle. You can continue using the App Tracker and ATS Checker within limits.

Do you offer a refund if I get a job fast?

If you secure a role within your first 14 days on the Operative plan, we'll refund that month's charge. Getting you hired is the mission.
