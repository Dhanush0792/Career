Frequently Asked Questions

Does Pro auto-renew or charge me automatically?

No. Pro is a one-time ₹199 prepaid pass valid for 30 days. You will never be auto-charged; when your pass expires, your account returns cleanly to Free without deleting any saved jobs or profile data.

What happens if I hit my 10 autofills or 2 exports on the Free plan?

Your monthly counters reset automatically on the 1st of each UTC month. You can continue viewing and editing your saved applications and profile anytime, or upgrade to Pro for immediate unlimited access.

What happens to my saved tracker jobs if Pro expires?

All your saved applications and profile data remain intact. You can still view, edit, delete, and export your existing tracker items; adding new entries above 20 requires Pro or freeing space.
