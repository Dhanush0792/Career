Why ATS Resumes Matter

Why is an ATS-friendly resume important?
An ATS-friendly resume is crucial because over 95% of Fortune 500 companies use Applicant Tracking Systems to automatically filter out unqualified applications before a recruiter reviews them. Resumes that lack proper keyword optimization, standard headings, or clean formatting are immediately parsed incorrectly and discarded.

Understanding how automated systems parse data is the first step toward getting more interviews.

How ATS Parsers Work

ATS software strips away styling, tables, and images, converting your document into plain text. It then scans this text for core factors:

  • Keyword Matching: Verifies if your resume includes skills matching the job description (e.g. "React", "Project Management").
  • Section Boundaries: Uses standard headings like "Experience", "Skills", "Education" to segment data correctly. Avoid creative headings like "Where I've Been" as they break parsing logic.
  • Contact Information: Extracts phone, email, and LinkedIn URLs to keep database profiles updated.
