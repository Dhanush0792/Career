Frequently Asked Questions

Does it automatically submit applications without my permission?
No. JobXApply is designed to fill out the tedious forms for you, but it will never hit the final "Submit" button. We believe human review is essential before firing off an application.
Is my personal data secure?
Yes. We use AES-256 local encryption. Your data is encrypted using your passcode on your device before syncing. We cannot read your plaintext career history or personal details.
Which browsers are supported?
The extension is fully compatible with Chromium-based browsers (Chrome, Edge, Brave). We also offer developer support for Mozilla Firefox.
